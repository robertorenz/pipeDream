Pipe Dream

The 1989 classic, rebuilt for the browser. 36 levels. Two views.

Lay pipe from the start piece fast enough to keep ahead of the flooz. Reach the distance for each level before the flooz spills out of an open end.

Best score: 0

Level Password

Passwords are shown every four levels. Enter one to jump straight there.

That password doesn't match any level.

How to Play

The goal

The flooz leaves the start piece when the timer runs out. Keep laying pipe ahead of it. Each level sets a distance: the number of pipe sections the flooz must pass through. Reach it and you clear the level; spill before then and it's game over.

Laying pipe

The next piece is at the bottom of the queue. Click any empty cell to place it. You can also drop a piece on top of one already on the board (as long as the flooz hasn't reached it) – it costs 50 points and a moment's delay.

Speeding up

Confident in your route? Press Space or the Flow! button to skip the countdown and make the flooz race. It stays fast for the rest of the level.

Scoring

  • +50 for every section the flooz fills
  • +500 for passing through both channels of a cross
  • +1000 for reaching the end piece
  • −50 for replacing a piece
  • −100 for each unused piece left on the board

Special pieces

  • Walls – nothing can be built there.
  • Reservoirs – fixed straights with a tank; take three times longer to fill. Handy for buying time.
  • One-way pipes – fixed straights with red arrows. Enter against the arrow and the flooz spills.
  • End piece – the flooz must enter through its opening. Reaching it ends the level with a bonus (you still need the distance).
